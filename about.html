Who We Are

How It Started

Founded in 2019 in a small garage in Woodstock, Cape Town by Israa Williams (ST10501763) during her studies. One oven, one sourdough starter, and a dream. We fermented our first loaf for 48 hours because we read that slow fermentation is better for taste and digestion - our customers agreed.

In 2020 we moved to 12 Albert Road Woodstock HQ. Bread baked at 4AM, pastries at 5AM, doors open 6AM. By 2022 we opened Claremont and Sea Point because Woodstock customers kept driving far. Now 3 branches, same recipe, no shortcuts.



Our Promise: Every loaf baked at 4AM, no commercial yeast, just natural starter. No preservatives ever.

What We Believe

  • Local Flour: We source from Western Cape farms only, supporting local agriculture.
  • Sustainable Packaging: All paper bags compostable, no plastic since 2021.
  • Zero Waste: Day-old bread donated to Woodstock homeless shelter, not thrown away.
  • Halaal Certified: All breads Halaal, most pastries Halaal except almond croissant.
  • Community: We hire from Woodstock, train youth in baking for free on Sundays.
  • No Preservatives: Just flour, water, salt, starter. That's it since 2019.

Our Journey

2019 - Garage Start: One oven in Woodstock garage, selling 10 loaves a day to neighbours.

2020 - Albert Road HQ: Moved to 12 Albert Rd Woodstock, first shop. 50 loaves daily.

2021 - Halaal Certified & No Plastic: All breads Halaal certified, switched to compostable bags.

2022 - 2 New Branches: Opened Claremont 45 Main Rd and Sea Point 8 Regent Rd.

2023 - 500 Loaves Daily: Hit 500 loaves a day milestone, team grew to 4 bakers + 3 baristas.

2024 - Best Bakery Award: Voted Best Artisan Bakery Cape Town - Cape Town Etc Readers Choice.

2026 - You Are Here: Still baking at 4AM, still 48hr fermentation, still Woodstock heart.

FAQ - Click to Open

What time do you open? +

Woodstock HQ: Mon-Sat 6AM-4PM, Sun 7AM-2PM. Claremont: Mon-Sat 6AM-3PM. Sea Point: Mon-Sun 6AM-5PM. Bread out by 6AM, pastries 6:30AM. We bake at 4AM daily.

Do you do custom cakes? +

Yes! From R350 Small (6 people), Medium R550 (12 people), Large R850 (20 people). Need 48hr notice. Halaal and nut-free available. Order via Enquiry page with date needed.

Gluten free options? +

We have gluten friendly sourdough on Fridays only, limited stock 20 loaves. Fermented 48hrs so easier to digest. Not 100% gluten free - made in same kitchen. First come first served from 6AM.

Where do you source ingredients? +

Flour from Western Cape farms, French butter for croissants, single origin Ethiopian beans for coffee. No preservatives, no commercial yeast. All natural starter from 2019.

Do you offer baking classes? +

Yes! Free youth training Sundays 9AM Woodstock HQ. Private sourdough classes R450 per person, 3 hours, you take home starter and loaf. Book via Enquiry page - select Baking Class.
