Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have Halaal options? +

Yes, all our breads are Halaal certified. Most pastries are Halaal except almond croissant which contains alcohol-based flavouring. Custom cakes can be made Halaal on request.

What time do you open? +

Woodstock HQ: Mon-Sat 6AM-4PM, Sun 7AM-2PM. Claremont: Mon-Sat 6AM-3PM. Sea Point: Mon-Sun 6AM-5PM. Bread comes out oven at 6AM, pastries at 6:30AM.

Do you deliver? +

Yes, free delivery within 5km of Woodstock for orders over R250. For Claremont and Sea Point we use Uber delivery at customer cost. Bulk orders over R1000 free delivery Cape Town CBD.

How to order custom cakes? +

Use enquiry form, select Custom Cake, enter quantity and date needed. Need 48 hours notice minimum. We will reply with cost and availability within 2 hours. Deposit 50% required.
